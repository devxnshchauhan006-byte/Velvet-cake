Our story

Baked slowly, finished beautifully

Velvet Cakes began in a tiny kitchen with one oven and a belief that celebration cakes should taste as wonderful as they look.

Every sponge is baked fresh to order using free-range eggs, real butter and responsibly sourced chocolate. Nothing artificial, nothing rushed.

Our bakers hand-decorate each cake, from delicate buttercream florals to sleek modern finishes.
